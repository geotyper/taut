taut amoeba

An amoeba as a bubble of the foam: its skin pulls taut and it keeps its area, but its edge reaches out for food. What it eats it grows by, living burns it away; at twice its size it divides, starved it dies. Amoebae do not eat each other, they push. cells

Left button divides an amoeba, right button or Shift lets it die. Keys: N start again, Space pause.

Sticking, the film between two amoebae pulls less than one on the water, so daughters stay together as one body. Sharing, two with a film between them even out their food, the faster the longer the film.

At 0 the edge counts for nothing and amoebae stick to it, as that spares them skin; at 1 it is as the water; above, it pushes them off.
